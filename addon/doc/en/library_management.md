# Library

Open the Library with `NVDA+Alt+G` then `M`. It opens straight away, and each tab loads its list the first time you open it, saying "Loading..." meanwhile.

The lists work like every list in Accessify Play: `Enter` plays a song or episode and opens an album, artist, podcast or audiobook, and the context menu holds what can be done with the item, with Remove from Library (or Unfollow Artist) in place of Save/Unsave. See [Lists and context menus](lists_guide.html).

## Shortcuts

These work in every tab:

| Shortcut | Action |
| :------- | :----- |
| `Enter` | Play a song or episode; open an album, artist, podcast or audiobook |
| `Ctrl+1` to `Ctrl+9` | Go to that tab, with the focus in its list |
| `Ctrl+Tab` or `Ctrl+PageDown` | Next tab |
| `Ctrl+Shift+Tab` or `Ctrl+PageUp` | Previous tab |
| `Alt+P` | Play |
| `Alt+Q` | Add to queue |
| `Alt+L` | Copy the Spotify link |
| `Alt+W` | Show the selected song's lyrics |
| `Alt+R` | Refresh the current tab |
| `Ctrl+N` | Create a new playlist |
| Applications key or `Shift+F10` | Context menu with more options |

These are the default keys. You can change them, and those of every other window: see [Customizing commands and shortcuts](customizing_commands.html).

The lists in the tabs come a page at a time, as many items as the Search Results Limit setting says. If there are more, the last item is "Load More": press `Enter` on it to add the next page.

When you remove an item from your library, its row disappears and the cursor stays where it was, on the next item.

## Tabs

### Manage Playlists

Choose a playlist in the **Playlist** box; its tracks appear in the list below. Each playlist is marked "Owned by You" or with the name of its owner.

* `Enter` on a track plays the playlist from that track.
* **Play Playlist**, or `Alt+P` anywhere on this tab outside the track list, plays the whole playlist. There, `Alt+Q` and `Alt+L` queue the playlist and copy its link.
* **Refresh Playlists** reloads your playlists and stays on the playlist and track you were on.
* **Edit Playlist Details**: change the name, description, and whether it is public or collaborative.
* **Delete Playlist** (your own) or **Unfollow Playlist** (someone else's), after asking you.
* **Remove Duplicates** removes extra copies of tracks that appear more than once, keeping the first copy of each. It tells you how many it found and asks before removing anything.
* **Clear Playlist** removes every track but keeps the playlist itself, after asking you.

In a playlist you own, you can also rearrange tracks:

* `Alt+Up Arrow` and `Alt+Down Arrow` move the selected track up or down.
* `Delete` removes the selected track, after asking you. If the same song is in the playlist more than once, only the copy you selected is removed.

The track context menu is a song's usual one; in your own playlists it ends with Remove Track from Playlist and Remove Duplicates.

**Creating a playlist:** press `Ctrl+N`, enter a name and, if you like, a description, and choose whether it is public. Public playlist starts checked, unless you turned off **New playlists are public** in the settings. A collaborative playlist must be private.

### Saved Tracks

Your Liked Songs. `Enter` plays a song, continuing with its album.

### Saved Albums

`Enter` opens an album's tracks.

### Followed Artists

`Enter` opens an artist's discography. Unfollow Artist in the context menu unfollows them.

### Top Items

Your most-played tracks or artists. Choose **Top Tracks** or **Top Artists** in **Show**, and Last 4 Weeks, Last 6 Months or All Time in **Time Range**; the list updates as you change them. The tab opens on Last 6 Months, or the period chosen under **Top Items in the Library opens on** in the settings. `Enter` plays a track, or opens an artist's discography.

### Saved Shows

The podcasts you follow. `Enter` opens a show's episodes.

### Saved Episodes

Episodes you saved to listen to later, showing the episode, its show and its release date. `Enter` plays an episode. View All Episodes of (the show), in the context menu, opens that podcast's full episode list.

### Saved Audiobooks

`Enter` opens a book's chapters.

### New Releases

Recent albums and singles. `Enter` opens an album's tracks, and `Alt+P` plays it. Spotify has announced that it will retire New Releases; if it does, the tab says so.

### Recently Played

What you listened to recently. `Enter` plays a track, continuing with its album.

## Adding the current track to a playlist

Without opening the Library: press `NVDA+Alt+G` then `A`, choose one of your playlists and press **Add to Playlist**. If the track is already in that playlist, you are told so instead.
