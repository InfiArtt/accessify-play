# Library Management

The Library Management Dialog (`M` in Command Layer) is the comprehensive control panel for your Spotify collection. It is organized into multiple tabs (pages) for easy navigation.

## Available Tabs

### 1. Manage Playlists
This is the main tab for managing your personal playlists.
*   **Playlist:** A Combobox to select which playlist you want to view or edit.
*   **Track List:** Displays the songs in the selected playlist.
*   **Play Button:** Plays the entire playlist.
*   **Edit Playlist:** Change the name, description, or privacy settings of the playlist.
*   **Delete/Unfollow:** Delete the playlist (if you own it) or stop following it (if it belongs to someone else).
*   **Reordering Tracks:** If you own the playlist, you can select a track and press `Alt+Up Arrow` or `Alt+Down Arrow` to move it. Press `Delete` to remove the track from the playlist.

### 2. Saved Tracks
A list of all your "Liked" songs.
*   **Action:** Press `Enter` to play.
*   **Context Menu:** Right-click to "Remove from Library".

### 3. Saved Albums
A list of full albums you have saved.
*   **Action:** Press `Enter` to view the tracklist of the album.
*   **Context Menu:** Option to remove the album from your library.

### 4. Followed Artists
A list of musicians you follow.
*   **Action:** Press `Enter` to view the artist's Discography.
*   **Context Menu:** "Unfollow" to stop following.

### 5. Top Items
Displays your personal statistics.
*   **Filter:** Choose between "Top Tracks" or "Top Artists".
*   **Time Range:** Select the period (Last 4 weeks, 6 months, or All time).

### 6. Saved Shows
A list of podcasts you follow.

### 7. New Releases
A curated list of new album or single releases from Spotify.

### 8. Recently Played
Your recent listening history. Useful for finding that song you just heard.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

*   `Ctrl+N`: Create New Playlist.
*   `Alt+P`: Play selected item.
*   `Alt+Q`: Add to Queue.
*   `Alt+L`: Copy Spotify Link.
*   `Alt+R`: Refresh data list.
*   `Ctrl+Tab`: Switch between tabs.
