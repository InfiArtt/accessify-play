# Lists and context menus

Every list in Accessify Play works the same way, whichever window it is in: Search, the artist window, album and playlist tracks, episodes and chapters, the Library, the queue, and Browse Categories and Featured Playlists.

## What a row says

A row gives the name first, then who made it, then more about it in brackets:

* **Song**: "Yesterday - The Beatles (2:05)". In an artist's window the artist is left out, unless someone else is on the song too. In the artist's All Tracks, the album comes before the length.
* **Album**: "Abbey Road - The Beatles (1969)".
* **Playlist**: "Today's Top Hits - by Spotify".
* **Podcast**: "The Daily - The New York Times".
* **Episode**: "Episode name - Podcast (2026-01-02, 45:00)". In the podcast's own episode list, the podcast is left out.
* **Audiobook**: "Book - Author". **Chapter**: "Chapter 1 (12:00)".
* **Artist**: the name only.

## What Enter does

* On a **song, episode or chapter**, `Enter` plays it. In an album, playlist, podcast or audiobook, playback carries on with the rest of it. Elsewhere (Search, the artist window, the Library's tabs), a song plays within its album.
* On an **album, playlist, podcast or audiobook**, `Enter` opens it in its own window. To play it without opening it, press `Alt+P`.
* On an **artist**, `Enter` opens the artist's discography.
* On **Load More**, `Enter` adds the next page.

## The context menu

Press the Applications key or `Shift+F10` on an item. The menu holds only what works for that item, always in this order:

1. **Play**, **Add to Queue** and **Copy Link**, with their shortcuts.
2. **Opening it**: View Tracks (album or playlist), View Episodes (podcast), View Chapters (audiobook), View Discography (artist), or View All Episodes of the podcast (episode).
3. **Save/Unsave**, or **Follow/Unfollow** for an artist or someone else's playlist. In the Library's tabs this is Remove from Library, or Unfollow Artist.
4. **Add to Playlist** (songs) or **Add Album to Playlist** (albums): a submenu of your own playlists. Songs already in the playlist are skipped.
5. **Follow/Unfollow Owner**, for a playlist someone else made.
6. For a song: **Show Lyrics**, **Go to Album** and **Go to Artist** (a submenu when there are several artists). For an album: Go to Artist. In an artist's own window, that artist is not offered again.
7. Last, what belongs to that window alone, such as Remove Track from Playlist in the Library.

"Save/Unsave" and "Follow/Unfollow" work like Like/Unlike: they save the item if it isn't in your library and remove it if it is, then tell you which they did.

Songs, episodes and chapters can be added to the queue. Albums and playlists are queued track by track. Spotify does not allow queueing a whole artist or podcast.

## Tabs

The windows with tabs switch them with the same keys: the types in Search, the tabs of the artist window and the tabs of the Library.

* `Ctrl+1` to `Ctrl+9` go to that tab, with the focus in its list.
* `Ctrl+Tab` or `Ctrl+PageDown` go to the next tab, and `Ctrl+Shift+Tab` or `Ctrl+PageUp` to the previous one. After the last tab comes the first again.

## Shortcuts

In every window with such a list, `Alt+P` plays the selected item, `Alt+Q` adds it to the queue, `Alt+L` copies its Spotify link and `Alt+W` shows a song's lyrics. These are the default keys; see [Customizing commands and shortcuts](customizing_commands.html) to change them.
